How to Play

RocketFun still uses the normal movement and shooting controls from Quake 4, but it adds new abilities and systems on top of them.

When you start playing, you can choose one of three heroes. Each hero has four abilities that use the Q, E, Z, and X keys.

To select a hero, open the developer console and type:

- hero 1 to play Vanguard
- hero 2 to play Striker
- hero 3 to play Medic

After choosing a hero, you can fight enemies normally while using your hero's abilities. Some abilities are locked until you reach a higher level.

Every time you kill an enemy AI, you earn XP and credits. XP helps you level up and unlock more abilities, while credits are used for the shop system.

You can check your current level and XP by typing `playerstatus` in the console.

You can also type `shop` to see the available items and their prices.

 The Three Heroes

 Vanguard

Vanguard is meant to be the tankier hero. His abilities focus on defense, protecting himself, and getting close to enemies.

- Q – Dash:** Quickly moves forward.
- E – Shield:** Gives temporary protection.
- Z – Shockwave:** Hits enemies around the player.
- X – Fortify:** Makes Vanguard harder to kill.

  Striker

Striker is the more aggressive hero. He focuses on moving quickly, dealing damage, and attacking enemies faster.

- Q – Speed Boost:** Increases movement speed for a short time.
- E – Berserk:** Gives an offensive boost.
- Z – Lifesteal:** Allows Striker to recover health from attacking.
- X – Rapid Fire:** Increases firing speed.

Medic

Medic is the support-based hero. His abilities focus on healing and keeping himself alive during fights.

- Q – Heal:** Restores health.
- E – Regeneration:** Heals over time.
- Z – Healing Pulse:** Creates a healing effect.
- X – Nanomachines:** Gives temporary damage immunity and 50 extra health.

 Leveling System

I added a leveling system similar to how champions level up in League of Legends.

Killing an enemy AI gives the player 100 XP. As the player gains XP, they level up and gain access to more abilities.

The XP requirements are:

 Level 2: 100 XP
 Level 3: 250 XP
 Level 4: 450 XP
 Level 5: 700 XP

You can type `playerstatus` in the console to see your current level and XP.
1. Launch Quake 4 using the RocketFun shortcut.
2. Start a single-player game.
3. Open the developer console and type `hero 1` to select Vanguard.
4. Use Q, E, Z, and X to try his abilities as they unlock.
5. Kill enemies to gain XP and credits.
6. Type `playerstatus` to check your level and XP.
7. Type `shop` to view the item shop.
8. Switch to `hero 2` to test Striker's abilities.
9. Switch to `hero 3` to test Medic's abilities.
